# The nurture sequence

Eight mails, running alongside the social calendar. They exist because the consultation booking rarely happens on first contact. The parent reads, thinks, and comes back. Email is where the coming back happens.

| # | Subject | The job it does |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | You don't have to figure this out alone | Opens the relationship and the free review. Applications for the 2027 intake are open. |
| 02 | 5 documents every application needs | The checklist. Miss one document, miss the intake. |
| 03 | WITS, UWC and UCT close in 44 days | First urgency mail. 30 September is closer than it looks. |
| 04 | This is what done-for-you actually means | Answers the value objection. Deadlines tracked, documents checked, status visible. |
| 05 | Bursaries are still open, for now | Funding runs alongside applications. Start both. |
| 06 | The Matric year, mapped | August to January, milestone by milestone. Orientation. |
| 07 | Have you applied yet? | A direct question, because September is nearly here. |
| 08 | September is closing season | 29 days. The last calm week to get it right. |

## The shape of the sequence

It moves from **reassurance** (01) through **competence** (02, 04, 06) to **urgency** (03, 07, 08), with funding (05) as the sideways step that re-engages anyone who has gone quiet on applications but is still worried about money.

That order matters. Leading with urgency to a cold parent reads as pressure selling. Earning trust first means the urgency mails land as helpful rather than manipulative, which is the difference between a booking and an unsubscribe.

## Writing a new one

Match the existing mails: a subject line that is a statement or a direct question, never a tease. One idea per mail. One CTA, the free review. Around 150 to 250 words.

Recount any day figure against the send date. Mail 03 says 44 days because of when it was written, not because 44 is a fixed number.

The HTML templates are in the social media content calendar zip under `emails/`, numbered to match this table. Reuse the structure rather than building fresh.

## Where the content comes from

The mails and the social calendar share source material deliberately, the Friday checklist carousel and mail 02 are the same five documents, the Monday countdown and mail 03 are the same deadline. A parent who sees both gets reinforcement rather than repetition, provided the framing differs.

Sunday's Q&A harvest is the main source of new mail topics. Questions that come up three times are a mail.
