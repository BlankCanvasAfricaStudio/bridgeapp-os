Overview

Traffic is not the problem. Measurement is.

Over almost eight months the property recorded 1 057 408 sessions and 15 851 057 events. It recorded zero key events and zero revenue, because the purchase event has never been installed. Every sale the business has made was counted by hand out of Peach.

Progress to 40 000

Banked against target. The bar is not a rendering error.

0.34%

137 sales banked of 40 000. Holding the deadline needs 2 667 a week for 15 weeks.

What one sale has to cost

The plan's own economics, never yet tested.

R50

R2 000 000 divided by 40 000 sales. At the measured R3.29 a click that needs 6.6% of everyone who clicks to buy.

Growth Engine 1

Commercial Sales

Buying attention from people who can pay. One real buy has ever run: a boosted post from 29 July that spent R1 617.57 and returned 491 link clicks. Everything known about paid performance comes from those eight days.

Current state

What the one real buy established.

R3.29

Cost per link click, measured. Click through rate 3.1% on 15 979 views. This is the only true cost figure the campaign holds.

+
Meta reaches the buyer60.7% women, and 35 to 44 was the largest age band. That is the parent who holds the card.
+
Organic works without spendOne reel reached 12 677 views, 87% of them non followers. Organic search brings 93 000 sessions a year for nothing.

What is not working

Each of these is measured, not inferred.

!
Paid has never run at scale52 people across eight months, from every paid channel combined. There is no performance history to optimise against.
!
The video is too long for its audience53 seconds of video against 7 seconds of average watch. Hook rate is healthy at 38%, hold rate is 7.6%. We are paying to deliver 46 seconds nobody sees.
!
Nearly half of all traffic is unattributedUnassigned is the single largest channel at 509 000 sessions. GA4 cannot say where it came from, so it cannot be repeated or defended.
~
TikTok reaches the student, not the buyer38 922 views and 5 194 likes, but 54% aged 18 to 24 and 97% returning viewers. Treat it as demand creation, never as a sales channel.

What is working

From the outreach report's own findings.

+
Face to face opens doors that email does notThe report is explicit that email-only outreach has a low response rate, and that personal visits are what generated the decision-maker engagement and the presentations.
+
Access is cheapR2 000 of Uber reached 27 schools, about R74 a school, and converted three of them into booked Grade 11 presentations.
+
Grade 11 is the right roomGrade 11 learners are choosing subjects and looking at university requirements now, which is when UniApply is worth buying rather than a year later under deadline pressure.

What is not working

Also from the report, plus what the numbers show.

!
No learner has been reached yetTwo weeks of work has produced access and zero registrations. That is not a failure at this stage, but it does mean the engine is entirely unproven commercially until the presentations run.
!
Three schools turned the team away at the gateMaragon Mooikloof, Tyger Valley and Amberfield require a confirmed appointment. Travelling to a school without one spends the voucher and returns nothing.
~
Sales support is the fragile linkThe report flags that opportunities get delayed or lost when support availability is uncertain, and asks for a faster handover once a presentation is secured.
~
20 a week has no basis yetThe 20 registrations a week objective is a target set before any presentation has been delivered. This week produces the first real conversion rate to test it against.

Enablement

What Gradesmatch has to switch on

The Growth Office can buy attention, book schools and write content today. What it cannot do today is prove any of it paid off. These six items are what turn this report from a description into a control panel, in priority order.

01
Fire a purchase event in GA4, with the transaction value and idNothing else on this list matters until this exists. Today the property holds 15 851 057 events, 0 key events and R0 of revenue, so no click has ever been tied to a payment. Send it from the Peach callback, dedupe on transaction_id.
Blocks everything
02
Give the Growth Office an automated feed of cleared transactionsCounting sales is now a tech function. The daily manual reconciliation out of Peach is not resourced, so without this or the purchase event above there is no way to record a single sale. It also does not scale: hand counting works for 137 sales and not for 40 000.
Blocks everything
03
Deploy the parent landing page on bridgeapp.co.zaPaid traffic currently has no dedicated destination. Every tagged link in the register has to be repointed before spend starts, and the conversion rate we measure is the rate of whatever page they land on instead.
Blocks the sprint
04
Expose a lead endpoint for the readiness trackerWithout it, sign ups are not captured, so click to sign up stays dark and only the end to end click to sale rate can be read. That halves what the sprint can learn.
Blocks the sprint
05
Filter automation traffic out of the property466 000 of 1 057 408 sessions, 44%, are bridge_client and Full Automation. Every rate in the property is diluted by them. Filters are not retroactive, so the sooner this is set the sooner the baseline is clean.
Degrades every number
06
Tag every outbound link, and keep tagging themUnassigned is the largest single channel at 509 000 sessions. Each untagged link adds to it, and traffic in that bucket cannot be repeated, defended or funded.
Degrades attribution

Counting a sale needs either item 01 or item 06. Neither exists yet and the manual reconciliation that used to cover the gap is no longer resourced, so as things stand the sprint that started on 24 August cannot record a single sale. Items 02 and 03 do not stop it, they narrow what it can learn.
