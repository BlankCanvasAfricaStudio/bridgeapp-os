# Weekly reporting

Reports exist to change next week's decisions. A report that does not lead to a different action is a status update, and status updates should be one line.

## Structure

```
## Week of [dates]

### Against target
| Stage | This week | Target (pro-rata) | Variance |

### What to boost Monday
[One post, with the numbers that chose it]

### What worked
[Content type, with the number that proves it]

### What did not
[Content type, with the number, and what replaces it next week]

### Decisions needed
[Only things the client must decide. If none, say none.]
```

## Pro-rating the monthly targets

The engine's monthly targets are 200 000 reach, 1 200 leads, 240 consultations, 60 conversions. Divide by the fraction of the month elapsed rather than by four, months do not contain four weeks, and comparing a five-week month against weekly quarters flatters or punishes unfairly.

## Reading the funnel honestly

The diagnosis lives in which stage is short, not in the headline number:

- **Reach short**, distribution problem. Boosting or posting frequency.
- **Reach fine, leads short**, the content is entertaining and not converting. Check the conversion-post floor of two per channel per week. This is the most common failure.
- **Leads fine, consultations short**, the booking step is broken. Test the link, the form, the calendar availability. Not a content problem, and rewriting content will not fix it.
- **Consultations fine, conversions short**, a sales conversation problem, or a price objection. Content cannot solve this either. Say so.

The instinct when numbers are down is to propose new content. Resist it unless the funnel says content is the issue, because replacing content that was working is how a good month becomes a bad one.

## On data sources

Social analytics are not currently connected to any automated pull. Numbers arrive as screenshots or CSV exports from Meta Business Suite, Instagram Insights and LinkedIn Analytics.

When numbers arrive as a screenshot, transcribe them into the report table so they are quotable later, and note the date range the screenshot covers. Screenshots without date ranges are not usable and should be sent back.

State plainly which figures are measured and which are modelled. The Google Analytics property currently records zero key events, so anything downstream of a click is a model.
