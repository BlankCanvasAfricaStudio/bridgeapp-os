# Cadence, rhythm and the deadline calendar

## Posting slots

These are fixed. They were set against when each audience is actually on the platform, and moving them breaks the comparison between weeks.

| Channel | Per week | Slots (SAST) |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 5 | Wed 18:00 · Thu 12:00 · Fri 17:00 · Sun 19:00 · Mon 07:30 |
| Instagram | 5 | Wed 16:00 · Thu 18:00 · Fri 16:00 · Sun 18:00 · Mon 16:00 |
| LinkedIn | 3 | Tue 08:30 · Thu 08:30 · Sat 10:00 |

Thirteen posts a week, roughly 30 per month across the consumer channels plus 12 on LinkedIn.

The Monday 07:30 Facebook slot is deliberately early: it is the week-opening deadline countdown, and it is meant to land while a parent is having coffee and thinking about the week ahead. Do not fill it with anything else.

Where a month turns mid-week, the Monday countdown may shift to Tuesday 07:30. That is the only slot allowed to move.

## The weekly rhythm

Each day of the week owns a job. This is what keeps a month of content from becoming thirty variations of the same post, and it means you can slot a new idea by asking "which day is this?" rather than starting from nothing.

| Day | Job | Why it sits there |
|---|---|---|
| **Wed** | Value and lead generation | Mid-week is when a parent has capacity to act on an offer. This is where the free review, the product walkthrough and the result cards go. |
| **Thu** | Story and proof | A named parent or student, a real outcome. Proof works after the offer has been made, not before. |
| **Fri** | Funding Friday, or a checklist | End of week is save-and-share behaviour. Bursaries and document checklists are the two things people send to other parents. |
| **Sun** | Q&A and recap | Sunday evening is family-planning time. Questions harvested here feed next week's content and the nurture sequence. |
| **Mon** | Deadline countdown | The week opens on urgency, with a real date and a real number of days. |

Two of each week's posts per channel must carry a conversion CTA. In practice that is usually Wednesday and one of Thursday or Friday.

## The deadline calendar

Countdown content is the highest-urgency thing on the account, and a wrong date does more damage than a missed post. Check every claim against this before it ships, and recount the days from the actual publication date rather than reusing a number from an earlier post.

| Closing date | Institutions |
|---|---|
| 30 September 2026 | WITS · UWC · UCT |

The set above is what the campaign has been running on. Before writing any new countdown, confirm the current list against the client's application calendar, because intakes shift and additional institutions get added through the season. If you cannot confirm a date, write the post without a specific number of days rather than guessing.
