# The twelve recurring post types

Every post in the campaign so far has been one of these. Working from the list is faster than inventing a format, and it keeps the month varied without anyone having to track variety by hand. Each entry says what it is for, which day it belongs to, and what the asset actually is.

---

### 1. Statement graphic
**Job** Land one uncomfortable truth. **Day** Mon or Wed. **Asset** Navy card, one word in yellow.

The whole post is a single sentence a parent recognises. "You're not behind. Here's your next move." "Most Matrics don't miss university on marks. They miss it on admin."

Works because it does not argue. It states, and the parent supplies the rest.

---

### 2. Deadline countdown graphic
**Job** Urgency, with a real date. **Day** Mon 07:30. **Asset** `campaign-application-calendar-feed.png`.

"WITS · UWC · UCT close 30 Sep. That's 44 days." Then what UniApply carries: the deadlines, the documents, the tracking.

Recount the days from the publication date every single time. Never copy a number forward.

---

### 3. Checklist carousel
**Job** Save-bait that also demonstrates competence. **Day** Fri. **Asset** White cards on navy, one item per slide.

The canonical one is the five documents: certified ID, results, proof of residence, parent income docs, APS breakdown. First slide carries the whole promise, "5 documents every application needs. Miss one, miss the intake.", so that the save happens before the swipe.

---

### 4. Explainer carousel
**Job** Teach one thing properly. **Day** Thu on Instagram. **Asset** Navy cards, yellow accents, six slides.

"APS, explained in 6 slides." What it is, how it is calculated, what the target course needs, what to do if you are short. Explainers earn the right to sell later in the week.

---

### 5. Story carousel
**Job** Proof through narrative. **Day** Thu. **Asset** Statement cards, or a real photo where one is cleared.

Two shapes work. The cautionary one: "He had the marks. He missed the date." Four slides, resolved by the free-review CTA, urgency without panic, and the resolution matters because a story that only frightens does not convert. The affirming one: "She got in. Here's the exact next move she made."

---

### 6. Q&A carousel
**Job** Answer real questions and harvest new ones. **Day** Sun. **Asset** Question card, answer card, alternating.

Questions come from the comments on the previous edition and from the CRM. Say so in the caption, "collected from your comments", because it makes the next round of questions arrive.

---

### 7. Funding Friday carousel
**Job** The highest-share content on the account. **Day** Fri. **Asset** Graphics-led, one bursary per slide.

Per bursary: eligibility, closing date, documents required. Pair it periodically with the one mistake that disqualifies most funding applications, which is what turns a useful post into a shared one.

---

### 8. Product walkthrough carousel
**Job** Show that "done-for-you" is a real product, not a promise. **Day** Wed. **Asset** Screenshots of the UniApply tracker UI.

One screen per slide: deadlines tracked, documents checked, status visible. This is the post that answers the objection "what am I actually paying for".

---

### 9. Result-card carousel
**Job** Make the Pathfinder quiz output desirable. **Day** Wed on Instagram. **Asset** Real result cards, one per slide.

Career matches, institution matches, readiness score. Three real results, not one, the variety is what makes a student want their own.

---

### 10. Story with poll or question sticker
**Job** Segment the audience and open DMs. **Day** Sun.

The poll version: "Applied yet?", "Not yet" voters get a DM with the booking link. Three frames: poll, proof, CTA. The question-sticker version: "Ask an advisor anything about applications." Best answers reposted Monday, unanswered ones routed to WhatsApp.

This is the cheapest lead source on the account because the segmentation is free.

---

### 11. Creator collab
**Job** Borrowed trust. **Day** Mon on Instagram, roughly monthly. **Asset** The creator's own screenshots.

A Tier-1 student creator shares their real Pathfinder results in their own words. Single CTA, tracked link. Do not script it, a scripted collab reads as an advert and loses the only thing it was bought for.

---

### 12. Recap carousel
**Job** Compound a month of proof into one ask. **Day** Month-end or month-open. **Asset** The month's best-performing cards, re-graded to brand.

Acceptances, reviews, quiz results, all into a single CTA. Cheap to produce and consistently one of the month's strongest performers, because it is the only post that shows scale.

---

## Choosing between them

If you are stuck, work backwards from the day. Monday is a countdown. Friday is a checklist or funding. Sunday is Q&A. That leaves Wednesday and Thursday, which are the offer and the proof, and if you have run the same offer twice in a row, switch to a walkthrough or a result card.

If the client asks for something outside these twelve, write it. The list is a starting position, not a fence.
