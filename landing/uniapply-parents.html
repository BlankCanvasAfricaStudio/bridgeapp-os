The reality

University applications are more complicated than they look.

Course

Questions

Before you decide.

Is this for me or for my child?
Both, but the account is yours. You see where your child stands, what still needs doing and every deadline coming. Your child does the parts only they can do, their marks, their subject choices, their preferences. The admin, the tracking and the payment sit with you.
What grade should my child be in?
UniApply is built for Matric: Grade 12 learners applying for the following year's intake. If your child is in Grade 11 or earlier, PathFinder is the right starting point. It helps them choose subjects that keep the degrees they want open, set an Admission Point Score (APS) target and build a realistic shortlist, so Matric year is execution rather than panic.
What exactly does UniApply include?
Applications submitted to up to five public institutions on your child's behalf, application fees covered up to R400, every document verified before it goes anywhere, live tracking on WhatsApp and SMS, one hour of consultation after submission, and bursary matching against their academic profile. R550 to start, then R150 a month for eleven months.
Can my child use BridgeApp on their own?
Yes, and most do the exploring themselves. But applications need documents, payment and someone chasing portals, which is why the account sits with you. In practice it works best when you both use it.
Does BridgeApp actually submit the applications?
Yes. That is the difference between UniApply and a guidance tool. We complete and submit each application, and we do it within 24 hours of your child's documents being verified.
Will it tell us the course requirements?
Yes, and more usefully, whether your child meets them. Every institution scores admission differently, so we calculate their position at each one rather than giving you a single generic number.
Can we compare universities on it?
Yes. Requirements, closing dates and application fees side by side, so the shortlist is a decision rather than a guess.
Is this a replacement for our school's counsellor?
No, and it isn't meant to be. Your school counsellor knows your child. BridgeApp handles the volume of information and the administration that no counsellor has time to do for every learner. Use both.
Can I cancel?
Yes, according to the applicable subscription terms, which are shown in full at checkout before you pay. [Legal to confirm exact wording, notice period and treatment of applications already submitted.]
Is my information safe?
Your details and your child's are processed under POPIA with your consent as the parent or guardian. Payments are handled by Peach Payments, we never see or store your card details.
