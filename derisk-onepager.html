The question

Can R2M buy 40 000 sales?

R2 000 000 divided by 40 000 is R50 a sale. That is the economics the plan assumes, and it has never been tested against a real transaction.

What we know is real: the July Meta buy spent R1 617.57 for 491 link clicks, so a click costs R3.29. For that click to become a R50 sale, 6.6% of everyone who clicks must buy.

At the 2% we are working to, a sale costs R164.72 and R2M buys about 12 100 sales, not 40 000. Reaching 40 000 at that rate costs R6.59m.

The point of the sprint

Nobody knows which world we are in: GA4 carries no purchase event, so no click has ever been tied to a payment. R10 000 answers it. R2M spent on the assumption does not.

The two levers

Cost per sale is the click divided by the conversion

Both have to move to reach R50. Conversion alone does not get there from where the click sits today.

Engine 1

Commercial Sales

Audience
The paying parent. Measured at 60.7% women, largest band 35 to 44, Gauteng first, then KZN and Western Cape.
Channel
All R10 000 into Meta, the only channel with real cost data. Four creative variants, including a 20 second recut of the July reel, which runs 53 seconds against a 7 second average watch.
Proves
Cost per click and click to sale. The two numbers the R2M decision rests on.
Engine 2

School Outreach

Audience
Grade 11 and 12 learners and the parents behind them, at 29 schools across Pretoria and Centurion. Selling direct to the family, not chasing school contracts.
Channel
Five days of visits on R2 000 of already unlocked fuel, a QR code unique to each school, and a take home slip for the parent who pays.
Proves
Sign ups per visit and cost per sale, on the same basis as paid, so the two can be compared on 6 September.
What we will know on 6 September
  • What a UniApply sale costs, both engines, measured not modelled.
  • Whether R2M can buy 40 000, or what it can actually buy.
  • How far the R3.29 click can be pushed down, across four variants.
  • Which engine is cheaper, and where the next rand should go.
  • Whether the measurement chain holds, from click to Peach to ledger. Nothing has confirmed that end to end.
The decision this buys
Release
At or near 6.6%. The plan works as written. Commit R2M against 40 000.
Re-cut
Around 2%. R2M buys roughly 12 100. Either the target moves or the budget does, decided on evidence.
Hold
Below 1%. Fix the click and the landing page before any further media is committed.
Needed from Gradesmatch dev by 24 August: the GA4 purchase event, the parent landing page live, and the readiness tracker lead endpoint. Without them the sprint still measures cost per sale, on manual Peach reconciliation, but attributes it more slowly.
